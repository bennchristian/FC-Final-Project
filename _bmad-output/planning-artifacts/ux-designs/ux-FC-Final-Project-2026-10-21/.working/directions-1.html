Witness Review: three visual directions (working mock, not final)

All three share the fixed decisions: serif headlines with one italic emphasis, a single dark red accent on off-white, one pill per screen, small mono labels, and an outline letter icon. They differ in register, density and layout. Each shows the opening screen and Post 03 of 10 on its Scores step, at 1280 by 800 with a 20px body.

Instrument Serif · Source Serif 4 · IBM Plex Mono

A Review read as a letter on good stationery. Everything is serif, the measure is narrow, and a ruled margin down the left holds the marginalia: the steps, the post being read, and a postscript that carries the standing reminder.

The sealed envelope sits beside the wordmark and marks every step, so the 2 Corinthians 3:2 idea runs quietly through the tool. It is the warmest and least dense of the three, which means long Evaluations scroll more.

Newsreader (display opsz) · Libre Franklin · IBM Plex Mono

The editorial register of a good newspaper: a masthead over a double rule, an asymmetric column grid, column rules, and score numerals set very large. The standing reminder runs as a strap under the masthead on every screen.

It is the densest of the three, so the Post and four Criteria sit above the fold together, and the Rubric reads like a set of results. The envelope works as a printer’s ornament: beside the wordmark and breaking the rule above the step index.

Quiet Room

Libre Caslon Display + Text italic · Atkinson Hyperlegible · DM Mono

The closest to House of Stewards: centred, spare, a lot of empty bone around a few things. Libre Caslon gives the headlines their warmth and Atkinson Hyperlegible carries the reading text, chosen because it holds up on a TV across a room.

The letter appears once, large and centred like a wax seal, and then only as the small mark on the current step. The reminder sits as a quiet folio at the foot of every screen. On the Scores step it gives up some calm for a two-column grid so four Criteria fit above the fold.
