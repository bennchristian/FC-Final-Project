# Living Letter — 2-minute video: script and cues

**Status:** draft 3 (2026-10-29). VO final; Bennett records about 2:00. 3.4's pause is shorter and the cues are
quicker. Next: the recording.
**Structure:** the five beats of the demo-day story guide: Hook (0:00–0:15) · The person and the problem
(0:15–0:35) · Show it working (0:35–1:30) · What users taught you (1:30–1:45) · Where it goes next
(1:45–2:00). Hard limit: **2:00**.
**Format:** the same as the 3-minute video (`docs/video/script.md`): motion design over the live Artifact,
with Bennett's voice-over recorded first. The VO is the clock: every cue fires on a spoken word.
**Demo Review:** the same Post as before and as "Try the demo" (FR-24): `artifact/sample-review.json`, the
public reel "Should a Christian attend a gay wedding?" (`witness-rubric@2 · persona-set@1 · prompts@2`).
Quotes from the Review in sections 3 are word for word.

## How to read this

Read the **VO** column aloud. **Bold words** are suggested cue triggers. The **Cue** column is a suggestion
(*idea:*) to keep, change or delete; write the final one as `on "<trigger words>" → <what happens>`.
**[pause ~Ns]** means leave silence while a quote is on screen to be read.

Motion vocabulary as before: **write-on**, **pop**, **zoom in / out**, **highlight**, **pan**, **cut**, **hold**.

Bennett is keeping every line and recording at about 2:00, so the read runs a little faster than 140
words a minute (about 155, which brings 285 words plus the pauses to about 2:00). The cues are quick to match: write-ons and highlights land on the trigger word
and clear within a beat, pans and zooms take about half a second, and nothing holds past its VO line except
the two reading pauses in 3.4 and 3.5. The motion pass fits the cues to the recording's real timing.

## 3. Show it working (0:35–1:30)

Three features, as a creator would use them: read a Post from its link, score it and see how it lands, then
fix it. The Review is replayed over the real Artifact (see Production notes).

| # | VO | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | It works by **pasting a link** to a reel or TikTok, and Living Letter **transcribes it.** Then, hit **Evaluate.** | *idea:* zoom into the Add Posts form, "Link to the Post" chosen, link in the field. Cursor clicks **Add Post** on "transcribes it", then **Send for review** on "Evaluate"; the envelope flies into the Mailbox; hard cut to the results. Blur the link after the click. |
| 3.2 | Every post is scored on **ten Criteria of good witness**, from **Scripture**, **Paul in Athens**, and **research** on how outsiders see Christians. | *idea:* quick pan down the Rubric Scores table, done by "research". |
| 3.3 | This one scored **ones** on **gentleness**, **welcome**, and **love.** | *idea:* Gentleness & Respect 1, Welcome over Judgment 1 and Love Made Visible 1 light up, one on each word. Cursor clicks Gentleness & Respect; its Evidence opens beside the table: "If you're a dude, why do you want to watch two guys kissing? That's very gay. Why are you gay?" |
| 3.4 | Then **seven simulated people** react. The Seeker felt **pushed away.** [pause ~2s] | *idea:* cut to Persona Reactions on "seven"; the Seeker is open; marker sweep on "he doesn't see people like me or my friends at all". |
| 3.5 | And the one believer on the panel? **Licensed to mock.** [pause ~5s] | *idea:* cursor picks the Emboldened Believer from "Read a Persona"; highlight "shows me that mocking people is a fair way to make a point". |
| 3.6 | Then a **gentler rewrite** keeps the **same conviction**, said with kindness, | *idea:* cut to the Gentler Rewrite with its changed words highlighted; marker sweep on "My answer is no, and I want to explain why as honestly as I can, whether you share my faith or not." |
| 3.7 | and **discussion questions** help a youth group talk it through. | *idea:* cut to Discussion Questions; marker sweep on "Imagine Jesus making a short video that answers 'Should a Christian attend a gay wedding?'" |

| # | VO | Cue |
|---|---|---|
