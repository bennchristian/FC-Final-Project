# Living Letter — 3-minute video: script and cues

**Status:** draft 2 (2026-10-26). VO edits from Bennett applied. Part B is rebuilt around a real Review.
Cues are Bennett's to write.
**Format:** motion design over the live Artifact (zoom in, zoom out, write-on text, highlights), with
Bennett's voice-over recorded afterwards. The VO is the clock: every cue fires on a spoken word.
**Visual source:** the Artifact itself, wherever possible: the envelope intro, the Review tab, the results,
and the Roadmap tab. Added graphics are only text write-ons and highlights laid over it.
**Demo Review:** Post 1 of `Living Letter Response 2.md`, exported 2026-10-26 (versions
`witness-rubric@2 · persona-set@1 · prompts@2`, model `claude-opus-5-5`). It is the Instagram reel that
asks "Should a Christian attend a gay wedding?". Everything quoted in Part B comes from that export.
**Scope:** this is the PRD §6.1 recorded video (Posts going in, how they are evaluated, the outputs,
the Roadmap tab).

## How to read this

Each section is a table. Read the **VO** column aloud. The **bold words** are suggested cue triggers:
the exact word on which something appears or moves. The **Cue** column is yours. Where it says
*idea:*, that is only a suggestion to replace or delete. Write each cue as
`on "<trigger words>" → <what happens>`.

In Part B, **[highlight: …]** marks a phrase from the real Review that is a candidate for highlighting.
Keep, swap or delete them. You choose the final phrases.

Motion vocabulary: **write-on** (text draws itself in), **pop** (fades or scales in), **zoom in / zoom out**
(on the Artifact), **highlight** (marker sweep or box around a phrase), **pan** (scroll to another part of
the page), **cut** (hard switch), **hold** (nothing moves).

The Artifact's own motion, usable as-is:
- **Envelope open:** the closed envelope fades in, the flap swings up, and the letter rises with "Start here".
- **Envelope exit (on Start here):** the letter slides back, the flap shuts, the envelope flies off the top,
  and the Review page rises in.
- **The letter's text:** "To the church in the 21st century, you who are living letters, known and read by
  everyone. 2 Corinthians 3:2-3" … "honest, loving feedback on how to make your witness even clearer."

## Production notes

- **Preloaded demo (5.1–5.3).** The Review is replayed, not run live. Post 1's saved results are loaded
  into the page, and clicking Evaluate plays the normal progress state for a few seconds before showing
  them, so it looks like a live run. This needs a recording-only replay path in the Artifact (or a separate
  capture build), so the public Artifact never shows it. Every on-screen result must be the saved text,
  word for word.
- **Outreach Plan (6.3).** The saved Review has no Outreach Plan (`"outreach": null`). It needs to be
  generated once in the Artifact for this Post and saved, so the shot shows real output. Until then,
  6.3 has no highlight.
- **Only Post 1 is shown.** The export also holds Post 2 (immigration). Drop it from the replay, or let it sit
  unopened below Post 1.
- **The creator.** The reel is a real person's public post. The URL on screen shows a post id, not a
  handle. Blurring the link field after 5.2 keeps the video about the post rather than the person.

## Open decisions

- **Paul line (3.4).** Spoken as 2 Corinthians 3:2 (NIV wording). The letter on screen paraphrases it
  ("known and read by everyone"). Either match the VO to the letter, or let the two differ slightly.
- **Part A timing.** Your edits bring Part A to about 1:01. If it runs long, trim 3.1 or 4.1 first.
- **Discussion question (6.5).** The standing question "How might Jesus have posted this?" appears in the
  Artifact as question 4, worded for this Post as "Imagine Jesus making a short video…". The VO paraphrases it.
- **The arrows in 5.6.** The text after each → is your summary, and it should appear as your overlay, not
  as the Artifact's words. The Review's own wording is "use 'gay' as a taunt and mock gay people" (Gentleness
  & Respect) and "reads as hostile even if it is meant as a joke" (Gentleness & Respect). "Makes me feel
  unsafe" is the Progressive Persona's line. If the cue highlights the Artifact's text rather than writing on
