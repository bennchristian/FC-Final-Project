# Living Letter — 3-minute video: script and cues

**Status:** draft 1 (2026-10-26). The VO is drafted; cues are Bennett's to write.
**Format:** motion design over the live Artifact (zoom in, zoom out, write-on text, highlights), with
Bennett's voice-over recorded afterwards. The VO is the clock: every cue fires on a spoken word.
**Scope:** this is the PRD §6.1 recorded video (Posts going in, how they are evaluated, the outputs,
the Roadmap tab).

## How to read this

Each section is a table. Read the **VO** column aloud. The **bold words** are suggested cue triggers:
the exact word on which something appears or moves. The **Cue** column is yours. Where it says
*idea:*, that is only a suggestion to replace or delete. Write each cue as
`on "<trigger words>" → <what happens>`.

Motion vocabulary to pick from: **write-on** (text draws itself in), **pop** (fades or scales in),
**zoom in / zoom out** (on the Artifact), **highlight** (underline or box around part of the screen),
**pan** (slide to another part of the page), **cut** (hard switch), **hold** (nothing moves).

## Open decisions

- **Paul line (3.4).** Drafted with 2 Corinthians 3:2, which is where the name comes from. If you would
  rather quote the call to gentleness, Colossians 4:6 ("Let your conversation be always full of grace,
  seasoned with salt") also fits.
- **Hook wording (1.1).** Kept close to yours. Whether "this person's a Christian?" lands as surprise
  depends on delivery; a pause before "this" helps.
- **Part A timing.** The draft reads at about a minute. If it runs long, cut 3.1 or shorten 4.4 first.
- **End card.** Tagline from the Artifact: "Learning to witness well in a world where our lives are the
  only gospel people know." Show it, say it, or leave it out.
